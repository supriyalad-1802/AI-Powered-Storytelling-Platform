ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 - Final Match Report
India vs New Zealand | March 8, 2026 | Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad

RESULT: India beat New Zealand by 96 runs

SCORECARD:
India: 255 for 5 in 20 overs
New Zealand: 159 all out in 19 overs

India won by 96 runs — the largest margin of victory in a T20 World Cup final ever.

INDIA BATTING:
Abhishek Sharma: 52 runs off 21 balls (6 fours, 3 sixes) — fastest fifty of the tournament, reached in just 18 balls
Sanju Samson: 89 runs off 46 balls — highest individual score ever in a T20 World Cup final, third consecutive fifty of the tournament
Ishan Kishan: 54 runs off 25 balls
Suryakumar Yadav (captain): dismissed by Neesham
Tilak Varma: lower order contribution
Hardik Pandya: lower order contribution
Shivam Dube: contributed with the bat in the death overs
James Neesham took 3 wickets for 46 runs for New Zealand

Powerplay: India raced to 92 for 0 in the first six overs — joint highest powerplay score in T20 World Cup history
Samson and Abhishek stitched a 98-run opening stand in just 43 balls
Samson and Ishan Kishan added a 105-run partnership
India reached 203 for 1 after 15 overs
India posted 255 for 5 — the highest total ever in a T20 World Cup final

NEW ZEALAND BOWLING:
James Neesham: 3 wickets for 46 runs

NEW ZEALAND BATTING CHASE:
