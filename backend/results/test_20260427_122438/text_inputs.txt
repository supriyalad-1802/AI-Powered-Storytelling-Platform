TATA IPL 2026 - Match 38
Lucknow Super Giants vs Kolkata Knight Riders
Venue: Bharat Ratna Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee Ekana Cricket Stadium, Lucknow
Date: April 26, 2026
Toss: Lucknow Super Giants won the toss and elected to bowl first.

Kolkata Knight Riders Innings: 155/7 (20 Overs)
The KKR innings started with an absolute collapse. Left-arm pacer Mohsin Khan unleashed terror with the new ball, severely strangling the KKR top order with hard lengths. Mohsin sent Tim Seifert, Ajinkya Rahane, and Rovman Powell packing inside the Powerplay, reducing the visitors to a disastrous 31 for 4. Mohsin Khan finished with a phenomenal 5-wicket haul (5 for 23 in 4 overs).

Adding to the drama, Angkrish Raghuvanshi was given out for a rare "obstructing the field" dismissal after trying to avoid a run-out.

However, Rinku Singh stepped up to execute one of the most inspiring rescue missions of his career. He absorbed the pressure and then unleashed sheer carnage in the final overs. Rinku mercilessly dismantled the bowlers, launching four monstrous sixes in a single over. He finished unbeaten on a spectacular 83 off just 51 deliveries (7 fours, 5 sixes), single-handedly dragging the Knight Riders to a highly competitive 155 for 7. Cameron Green provided brief support with 34 off 21 balls.

Lucknow Super Giants Innings: 155/8 (20 Overs)
Chasing 156, LSG started steadily but lost wickets at regular intervals against the spin of Varun Chakravarthy (2/33) and Vaibhav Arora (2/24). Captain Rishabh Pant top-scored with 42 off 38 balls, while Aiden Markram chipped in with 31 off 27.

The drama reached its absolute peak in the final over, bowled by Kartik Tyagi. LSG needed 15 runs from the last five deliveries. The equation eventually boiled down to 7 runs required off the final ball of the innings. In a moment of pure disbelief, tailender Mohammed Shami hammered a stunning six over the long-off boundary, tying the game at 155 for 8 and sending the Ekana crowd into absolute delirium. We were headed to a Super Over!

The Super Over:
Lucknow batted first in the Super Over. KKR Captain handed the ball to veteran maestro Sunil Narine, who delivered an absolute masterclass under the highest possible pressure. Narine shattered Nicholas Pooran's stumps on the very first delivery for a golden duck. After Pant managed a single, Markram attempted a massive heave, only to be caught at the long-on boundary. LSG lost both their wickets and only managed to score 1 run!

Chasing a target of 2 runs, the man of the hour, Rinku Singh, casually dispatched the very first delivery for a boundary to seal a breathtaking win for KKR.

Result: Kolkata Knight Riders won the Super Over.
Player of the Match: Rinku Singh (83* and 4 crucial catches in the outfield).
